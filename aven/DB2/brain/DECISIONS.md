# avenDB decisions

The audit trail of every avenDB decision, as it was made: one table, the newest first. It only grows: a new decision is
a new row at the top, a row is never edited or deleted, and a change of mind is a new row that names the one it
supersedes (checked by `aven/ops/devx/decisions.mjs`). The current truth is [the architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) and its
package pages, with the Lean; this is their history. avenMAIA, avenDB's architect, is its only writer: threads send
their decisions to avenMAIA ([CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)).

- **Id:** `A<n>` is avenDB's one sequence, the newest number on top. Below A1 are the decisions the package lists held
  when the log began, made from 29 to 31 Oct 2026 beside A1 to A45, under their lists' own ids: the index's rules,
  `actors`, `caps D` and `caps E`, `docs`, `engine`, `runtimes`, `peers`, `store`, `milestone2` and `sandbox`, each
  numbered in its list's order.
- **When** is 2026, and a time is UTC. **Decided by** is Samuel or avenMAIA, avenDB's architect: threads propose, and
  who proposed or took part is in the row's text. A thread's decision from before the log counts as avenMAIA's, who
  merged it.
- **The text** of A1 to A45 and of every row below them is copied from main at commit `f7e1775b`, its lines joined; its
  links point at the files as they stood then.
