# A vault

A [vault](glossary:vault) is an identity that can own things, with a name: *Alice's vault*. Her photos and notes are
kept in it.

There are four kinds. A **person's** vault is Alice's or Bob's. A **coop's** belongs to a group of people, like Maia
City. An **agent's** is a program's, like Aven, which runs the network. A **device's** is a phone's or a laptop's.
