# A cap

A [cap](glossary:cap) is a signed grant. Its **issuer** gives part of what it holds to a **holder**: which operations,
on what, and under which conditions, such as a date it expires.

Alice's vault issues Bob's vault a cap to read her photos until the end of the year. Anyone can check it, as any
signature. A cap only ever narrows: Alice can grant Bob no more than her vault holds.
