# Vaults own vaults

A vault's owners can be vaults too. Alice's vault owns **Maia City**, a coop's vault. Maia City owns **Aven**, the
agent's vault that runs the network.

A vault **owns** what is directly below it, and **governs** everything below it: Alice's vault governs Maia City and
Aven. Authority runs only down. Aven governs nothing above it.
