# A passkey

A **passkey** is a key that Alice's phone or browser keeps for her, and unlocks only with her fingerprint, her face or
her PIN. Phones and browsers make and keep passkeys in the same standard way.

Alice's **owner key**, the key that owns her vault, comes from her passkey. So she has nothing to write down: on a new
browser, she signs in with the passkey, and that browser is added to her vault as one of its devices.
