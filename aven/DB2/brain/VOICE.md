# The language

How avenBRAIN talks.

## The rules

- **From the ground up.** For someone with no background: each slide stands on the ones before it.
- **One concept per slide,** and at most one new term on it, linked to the glossary (`[vault](glossary:vault)`).
- **No term before it is introduced.** A slide may use only the words of the slides before it, and everyday words.
- **Plain, short sentences,** one idea each. Concrete before abstract: Alice and her key before "a principal".
- **Exact.** Every claim says what the specs mean, in their words ([`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md)); a simplification
  says so ("That comes later"), it never says something false.
- **English.** The brain is in English.
- **"Signs for" is a device key's** (slide 7); an owner key **governs** its vault.
- **One metaphor per concept, its own.** Draw each concept as the object that explains it (STYLE.md, "Metaphors"):
  a signet for a key, a strongbox for a vault, a robot helper for an agent. Not one picture for everything.
- **No attributions in the text:** no names and dates of who decided what. The brain says what is.

## The story map

Each package's page opens with its story: the concepts in the order a reader meets them. avenID's is in
[`id/README.md`](id/README.md): nine slides, from "A key signs" to "A cap", agreed.
