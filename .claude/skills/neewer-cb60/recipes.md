# Lighting with the CB60 in Samuel's room

The settings are starting points (estimates) — check every one against the zebras or false colour on camera.

## Before every set-up

- The camera's white balance on a fixed Kelvin, never auto; the shutter at 1/50 (25p) or 1/100 (50p).
- Run the light from the phone at the camera; keep "talk" and "mood" in the app's two control-group slots.
- A sandbag on the stand's leg under the head.
- The white walls fill every shadow: black cloth on the shadow side keeps the face's shape (`gaffer`, `setups.md`).

## The modifiers it takes

| Modifier | Spread | Softness | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bare head | about 110–120° | hard, a 4 cm source | bouncing off a wall or the ceiling |
| The stock reflector | a ~25° hotspot and a dim spill | hard, crisp shadows | throw, a rim, hard "sun" (about 10× the bare head on its axis) |
| RF1 reflector | 55° | hard | a wider version of the stock one |
| Honeycomb grids (with their 7" reflector) | 10°, 30°, 50° | hard | a spot with no spill on the white walls |
| Snoot | about 10–20° | hard | a hair light, an accent on the background |
| Beauty dish (55 cm) | about 60–90° | semi-soft, punchy | a beauty key |
| Softbox, parabolic or octa (65–90 cm) | about 90–120° (50–60° with a grid) | soft at 0.6–1.5 m | **the key**; costs 1–2 stops |
| Lantern (50–90 cm) | all round | very soft, wrapping | spills everywhere in a small white room |
| Umbrella (through the umbrella hole) | about 120–160° | soft, lots of spill | a fast fill |

## Seven recipes

1. **The key through a softbox at 45°** — soft, flattering, still shaped. A 65–90 cm softbox with both diffusion layers,
   45° off the camera axis, 0.8–1.2 m from the face, its centre 30–50 cm above eye level, tilted down 20–30°; aim it a
   little in front of the face so its edge lands on it (feathering). CCT 5600 K (or the room's), G/M 0, from 40 %; skin
   highlights at about 60–70 % IRE.
2. **A bounce off the white wall or the ceiling** — soft, natural fill. The reflector on, throwing a tight patch on the
   wall 0.5–1 m in front of and beside the subject at face height; for the ceiling, aim between the camera and the
   subject (straight above the head darkens the eyes). 80–100 %. Check the paint's colour on camera; correct with G/M.
3. **A hair or rim light** — the subject off a dark background. Behind, opposite the key, at about 2–2.2 m, aimed at the
   back of the head and the shoulders; the reflector, a snoot or a grid; flagged off the lens. 5–15 % (full is 8,000
   lux at 1.5 m); 6000–6500 K for a cool edge, 3200 K for a lamp behind. **With one light:** the CB60 as the rim, the
   window as the key.
4. **Motivated "window" daylight** — the scene reads as day, the shadows fall as the window would cast them. On the
   window side at about the window's top (2 m), through a large softbox or the sheer curtain; for hard sun, the bare
   reflector's beam from as far as possible. 5600 K, 70–100 %; the camera at 5600 K for neutral, 4300–5000 K to keep the
   window cooler than warm lamps. **Our window looks north:** big diffusion and 6000–6500 K.
5. **A warm match to the Edison bulb** — the light becomes the bulb's, for evening and night. From the bulb's direction
   and height, bounced or through a small softbox. 2700 K is its warmest: for a filament's 2200–2700 K, switch to HSI,
   hue 25–35°, saturation 30–50 %, matched by eye with the bulb in frame; 5–20 %. The camera at 3200–3600 K for natural
   skin with a golden bulb, at 5600 K for an orange, cosy look.
6. **A coloured wash on the background (HSI)** — depth, and teal against a warm key. The subject at least 1–1.5 m from
   the wall; graze the wall from the side or from below, flagged off the subject. Hue 200–230 (teal and blue), 270–300
   (purple) or 15–30 (sunset); saturation 80–100 %; 20–60 % (blue needs more).
7. **The effects** — TV screen from in front and low, through diffusion or bounced, 20–50 %; Lightning from high on the
   window side at 100 % with the reflector (and thunder in the mix); Candlelight low and bounced at 30–60 % for a fire;
   Explosion for one blast; Cop car, Paparazzi, Party, Hue loop for story beats and intros. In B-roll and intros, not
   under a long talk. Film a 10 s test at 25p first, and avoid fast strobing in what is published.

## Safety and care

- **Heat:** its front, fins and body reach up to 60 °C — keep **at least 0.5 m from the bed, the curtain and paper**,
  never aim it at anything that burns, let it cool about 2 minutes before handling the front; in a closed softbox,
  watch its temperature (hold HSI/🌡 for 3 s).
- **Air:** never cover the side vents, the slots underneath or the rear grille.
- **The umbrella hole** only for umbrella shafts, through the reflector's slot first; tighten the angle lever and
  sandbag the stand — an umbrella is a sail.
- **The cable:** switch off and unplug before fitting or removing a modifier or the cap; run the 4 m cable down a leg
  with a loop at the inlet, out of the way; pull the plug, never the cable; never use a damaged cable.
- Don't stare into the LED; clean only with a blower or a dry cloth; don't open it; keep 20 cm between the antenna and
  your body for long use.
