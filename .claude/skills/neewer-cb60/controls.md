# Operating the CB60 RGB

## The back panel

Facing the back: the LCD at the top; under it a row of five buttons (left to right) **∨ · ∧ · CCT · HSI/🌡 · FX/CH**; the
**BT ↔ 2.4G** slide switch and the red power LED lower left; the power inlet in the middle; the **push-and-turn knob**
lower right; the antenna stub top left. The O/I rocker is underneath, near the back.

| Control | Short press | Long press | Turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| ∨ / ∧ | brightness −/+ 1 % | ramps fast | — |
| CCT | the CCT mode | **5 s: resets the app pairing (Infinity)** | — |
| HSI/🌡 | the HSI mode; also leaves the temperature view | **3 s: shows its inside temperature, °C and °F** | — |
| FX/CH | the effects mode | in 2.4G mode: the channel number flashes, ready to set | — |
| Knob | chooses which value flashes (CCT ↔ G/M; saturation ↔ hue); in FX, the next effect | — | changes the flashing value, or the effect |
| BT ↔ 2.4G | left: app mode (the Infinity icon flashes); right: 2.4G channel mode | — | — |

**Before switching on:** take the black protective cap off the front.

## The modes

- **CCT:** press CCT. Press the knob until the Kelvin flashes, turn it (2700–6500 K, in 100 K steps); press again until
  G/M flashes, turn it (−50…+50 — check which way is green on a white card). ∨/∧ for brightness.
- **HSI:** press HSI. Press the knob until S (saturation) or H (hue, 0–360) flashes, turn it. ∨/∧ for brightness. A
  fully saturated colour is far dimmer than white (red and green a fraction, blue least).
- **FX:** press FX/CH; press or turn the knob to step through the effects; ∨/∧ for brightness. No speed or colour
  setting per effect on the unit.
- There is no gel, XY or source-match mode — CCT, HSI and the effects only.
- **The display:** CCT shows the brightness, G/M and the Kelvin's first two digits ("56" + a printed "00K"); HSI shows
  brightness, S and H; FX shows a grid of the 17 effect icons. "CH." and a number in 2.4G mode.
- **The fan** runs low whenever it is on and speeds up when hot (users report it quiet even at full); no silent mode.
  Above 70 °C inside, it protects itself.
- **Memory:** none documented on the unit — test whether it keeps its last setting after power-off.

## The 17 effects

Lightning · Paparazzi · Defective bulb · Explosion · Welding · CCT flash · Hue flash · CCT pulse · Hue pulse · Cop car ·
Candlelight · Hue loop · CCT loop · Intensity loop · TV screen · Fireworks · Party. There is no "fire": Candlelight is
the closest; Explosion is a one-off blast. Their timing isn't published — film each against a white card at 50 fps to
copy it into a world shot.

## The NEEWER app (Infinity, Bluetooth)

1. Install "NEEWER" (iOS "NEEWER Studio"); Bluetooth on (and, on Android, location).
2. Slide BT ↔ 2.4G to the left: the Infinity icon flashes.
3. Paired before, or not found? Hold CCT for 5 s to reset.
4. In the app choose **NEEWER Infinity** as the connection, tap "+", pick the CB60 RGB, OK; tap it to open it.
5. Its tabs: CCT (brightness, Kelvin), HSI (a colour wheel with an RGB read-out, hue, saturation, brightness), SCENE.
   Favourites and two control-group slots keep settings ("talk", "mood"); groups of up to 8; firmware updates over the
   air (⚙ → Version → Firmware upgrade). Range about 15 m.

**Run it from the phone at the camera** — Samuel sets the light from the frame, on his own.

## 2.4G sync

Slide the switch right, hold FX/CH, turn the knob to a channel (CH0–CH99): every light on the same channel follows the
one you change, within 20 m (Neewer lists the SL90, RGB1, RGB C80 and RGB 1200 as partners). The app can't control it in
this mode.
