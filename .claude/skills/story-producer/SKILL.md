# Story producer

One skill for every story maiaCITY tells — on the page and on screen. The same few laws carry a journal post, a
90-second reel and a title card; the sub-skills below are how each is applied.

## The four laws (never break them)

1. **The hook goes as extreme as the truth allows.** The biggest, strangest end state the facts can carry, set
   against the reader's biggest belief. A mild hook is a lost reader. → `hooks.md`
2. **One curiosity loop, open from the first line to the last.** A single arching question the reader cannot leave
   without the answer to — held open by progress, conflict and doubt, answered in the last lines, fast. → `arc.md`,
   `retention.md`
3. **The biggest possible transformation for the reader.** Decide *from* (what they believe arriving) and *to* (what
   they leave with) before a word is written. The larger the distance between the two, the stronger everything else
   gets. → `arc.md`
4. **Lead with the pain.** Every intro educates first: what is wrong, concretely, in the reader's own life — then
   the new world. The reader must feel the wrong before the right can land. Pain, never dread. → `arc.md`

And always: **every fact true** (nothing invented about Samuel's life, the readers, attempts or results), **full
conviction** (a vision being built, not hoped for), **contrast on every axis** (a high only reads against a low).

## The order of work

Story before production, always:

1. **Transformation** — from → to, as far apart as the truth allows (`arc.md`).
2. **The arching question** — the one question the whole piece answers (`arc.md`).
3. **Hooks** — write at least ten, score them, keep one (`hooks.md`).
4. **The arc** — the pieces that move toward the answer, the lows set against the highs, the intensity curve
   (`arc.md`, `retention.md`). Show it to Samuel before drafting.
5. **Draft** — in the voice, with the facts (`writing.md`). For a film: the script, then the takes (`sound.md`).
6. **Production** — shot list, storyboard, score, shots, render (`filming.md`, `sound.md`, `pipeline.md`).
7. **Audit** — the checklists in `writing.md` (post) or `pipeline.md` (film).

The derivatives of a finished day (posts per platform, title cards, scheduling) are the `content-derivatives`
skill; their hooks and title cards still follow `hooks.md`.
