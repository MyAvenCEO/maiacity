# Security

Found a weakness in maiaCITY (the site at maia.city, its API, avenDB and its server, or The OS)? Please tell us
privately, never in a public issue, pull request or discussion: this repository is public.

## How to report

- **GitHub:** [report a vulnerability](https://github.com/MyAvenCEO/maiacity/security/advisories/new) (private
  vulnerability reporting), or
- **Email:** maiacityguardian@gmail.com

English or German. Say what is affected, how to reproduce it, and what it lets someone do. We answer as soon as we
can, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you when the fix is out, if you want.

## Please

- Test only against your own accounts and vaults, or a local build; never with load against maia.city, api.maia.city
  or avendb.maia.city.
- Don't read, change or keep anyone else's data.
- Give us time to fix it before you speak about it.

The same contacts are at [maia.city/.well-known/security.txt](https://maia.city/.well-known/security.txt).
