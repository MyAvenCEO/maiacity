<!--
HOW TO WRITE ONE OF THESE

Same story machine as the Inspire me report cards:

  HOOK → CONTEXT → PROBLEM → INTENTION TO OVERCOME → OBSTACLE → SOLUTION

Every section follows the one before it either *because of it* or *against it* —
the therefore / but rhythm. Never write those words. Let the sentences carry it:
a claim, then the thing that breaks it, then what that forces.

The article delivers one transformation. Decide the from → to before writing:
  from: <the belief the reader arrives with>
  to:   <what they leave with>
The last paragraph lands the "to". If the piece could end three paragraphs
earlier without loss, cut those three paragraphs.

Rules of thumb:
- Short paragraphs, 1–3 sentences. Air on the page.
- Real numbers from the thing you actually built. No rounded-off adjectives.
- One image per beat, not per paragraph.
- No filler: delve, leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer.
-->
