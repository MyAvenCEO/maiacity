This afternoon I put 100 hearts into Solar and got 1.11 pieces of it.

Mara, the test citizen who founded Solar on my test copy of the game, paid less than half as much for each of hers.

Nobody sold me anything. Nobody haggled. There is no market in avenCITY Sandbox 2, and no one sets the price by hand. The price was waiting for me, and it will be higher still for whoever comes after me.

## Ownership of something that does not exist yet has no price

On [Day 09](/blog/day-09-hearts-and-minds/) I described the pair: hearts to pay with, minds to own with. You invest hearts into a coop, and you receive its minds, your share of owning it.

On [Day 11](/blog/day-11-the-earth-in-cards/) I founded a Bakery on the coast of Portugal. Mara founded Solar next to it.

That leaves the hard question. How many minds is a heart worth?

A new coop has no revenue and no customers. It has a pitch and one person's stake. Price its ownership too cheap for too long, and the thousandth backer gets the same deal as the first, who took all the risk. Price it too high at the start, and nobody goes first.

## The first answer counted hearts, and burned them

The first coop cap table went into the sandbox early on its second morning. Two hours later coops were real: you could found one on a card and invest in it.

That first version made three choices. Within the same morning I undid two of them. The third took longer.

The first was a daily cap: nobody could invest more than a set amount a day. It lasted twenty minutes. A cap does not protect a coop. It only slows down the people who believe in it most.

The second was the unit. Milestones were counted in hearts raised, so every milestone meant a different number of minds. Half an hour later I turned it around. Milestones are now counted in minds, the thing you actually own, and the hearts follow from them.

The third was the burn. Investing burned your hearts. They were taken out of the world, the coop received a record of ownership, and half of the new minds went to the investor and half to the coop's own treasury.

## The price climbs with a rule older than money

Here is the rule, as it stands now. Picture a staircase whose steps get taller the higher you climb.

A coop grows through 49 milestones. At each one its total supply of minds reaches the next number in a sequence: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34… Each number is the sum of the two before it. Leonardo of Pisa, called Fibonacci, brought it to Europe in 1202, and it turns up in sunflower heads and pine cones. It grows slowly at first, then very quickly, and never in a jump.

Every milestone releases the difference to the one before. Milestone 6 releases 5 minds. Milestone 9 releases 21.

The price is even simpler. Ten hearts per mind at milestone 1. Twenty at milestone 2. Ninety at milestone 9. That step of ten went in before lunch on the second day, and it has not changed since.

![The emission tab: every milestone, the minds it releases, the price and the total supply.](/day-12-the-first-believer-pays-least/emission.jpg)

Everyone inside one milestone pays the same price, like a funding round. The first mind of a coop costs 10 hearts. The last ones, at milestone 49, cost 490. By then there are 8 billion minds, and filling every milestone of a single coop would take 3.7 trillion hearts.

The milestones group into five phases of ten: IDEA, TEST, BUILD, SCALE and HERO. Every coop in the sandbox today is still an idea.

## Your stake carries you through the first five milestones alone

Now the founding stake from Day 11 makes sense.

When I founded the Bakery, my 500 hearts went through the milestones one after another. Milestones 1 to 5 cost 290 hearts and gave me 8 minds. The remaining 210 bought 3.5 minds at milestone 6, at 60 hearts each.

11.50 bakery☉, for 500 hearts. An average of 43.5 hearts per mind.

By the time Lea, Tomas, Ines and Rui had backed Solar, it had raised 2,300 hearts and reached milestone 9. My 100 hearts bought minds at 90 each.

![Before investing: 100 of my hearts would buy 1.11 solar☉ and become 100 maiaHEARTS in Solar's treasury.](/day-12-the-first-believer-pays-least/before-investing.jpg)

That is the whole reward for believing early. Not a bonus, not a special deal. The price at the moment you arrived.

## Burning hearts was the wrong answer

The burn was the part that never fit.

The people who believed in a coop gave up their hearts, and those hearts were gone. The coop got a cap table and nothing to build with. The bakery had backers and no ovens. And half of every new mind sat in the coop's own treasury, owned by the coop, which is to say by nobody in particular.

So I changed both.

Hearts are no longer burned. They are converted, one for one, into maiaHEARTS, the city's common currency, and they land in the coop's treasury. A hundred of my hearts became a hundred maiaHEARTS in Solar's treasury. Nothing disappears and nothing is skimmed.

The minds all go to the people who invested. The treasury does not need them. It already holds the money.

![My ledger: the hearts I minted, the hearts that became maiaHEARTS, and the minds I received back.](/day-12-the-first-believer-pays-least/ledger.jpg)

It all happens as one step. Your hearts leave, the maiaHEARTS arrive, your minds appear, and the coop's total moves on. If anything fails halfway, nothing happened at all.

Imagine the morning Solar's first dome factory opens. The machines were paid for with maiaHEARTS from its treasury: the hearts of its backers, turned into the city's money. Everyone who put hearts in owns a piece of every panel that leaves the building, and the earliest owns the most per heart.

## Where the hearts are going

This is my test copy of the sandbox this evening.

![The coops: Solar at milestone 9, Bakery and HempStone at milestone 7.](/day-12-the-first-believer-pays-least/the-coops.jpg)

Solar has raised 2,400 hearts from six backers, and its treasury holds 2,400 maiaHEARTS. Bakery holds 950. HempStone holds 750. Every one of those maiaHEARTS started as somebody's own hearts, minted every two minutes, owed to nobody, and sent to something they wanted to exist.

Imagine this at the scale of a city. A million people, each minting the same hearts and each deciding for themselves where to send them. The coops people believe in get their treasuries filled. The people who believed own them. And the price of that belief is set by the same rule for everyone, on the day they arrive.

That is how a heart becomes a maiaHEART. The rule is public, the ledger is shared, and the sandbox is open.

On the live planet no coop has sold its first mind yet. The first believer there pays 10 hearts.
