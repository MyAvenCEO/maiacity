# Journal storytelling

Everything we know about telling a maiaCITY story — the hook, the arc and transformation, retention, the writing
style, and for films the shots, the sound and the pipeline — now lives in one skill:
`.claude/skills/story-producer/` and its crew (start at its `SKILL.md`; the journal voice and the post checklist are in
`storyteller/writing.md`, the hook in the `hook-writer` skill, the arc in `storyteller/arc.md`, tension and release in
`storyteller/retention.md`, scene by scene in `storyteller/scenes.md`).

`TEMPLATE.md` is still the empty frontmatter skeleton for a new day.
