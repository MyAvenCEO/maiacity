# The test story

**Step: Idea.** The brainstorm pad: links, concepts, fragments. Nothing here is decided; what survives goes into the
Hook, the Journey and the Writing.

**What Day 0 is for:** a test of the whole pipeline, from this pad to a 4K upload on YouTube (title, description,
thumbnail, the master file), and of the new Stories board. Its film is a **10-second trailer intro** shot in the Isar
world: a mood, a place, a name, **no spoiler** of anything to come. It is uploaded once to test the delivery, then
deleted.

## What we take from the two videos

**From the devlog:**
- One goal for the episode, said in the first breath, with the place and the weather ("rainy Hamburg").
- Plan → build → **show it working**, piece by piece (the test spheres, then the bunting).
- The clock as the spine: "a few days later", "8:00 on Tuesday", "20 to 6, dinner".
- A deadline makes the to-do list a story ("two more days, then the Alps").
- Life between the work: the run while the sun is out, the salad from the balcony.
- The reasons said aloud, the honest scope ("next iteration"), the demo at the end, the supporters by name.

**From LifeOfRiza:**
- Every idea written down at once; pre-production is the hard part and the one that saves the rest.
- Pick stories with a full arc already in them (a marathon, a transformation).
- **Plead your case:** nobody knows us — the context makes them care.
- Style is inspiration times constraints (alone on a tripod → many angles → her signature).
- A reason for every camera move; most of the look made in camera (light, set, wardrobe colour).
- Look effortless; let them live in the story.

## Our format, in one line

**Half devlog, half a life based on a true story.** The city being built, seen working — inside a cinematic story of
the man building it. Voice-over and talking to camera, mixed. The character: a leader setting out with others, the
energy of a movement (King's "I have a dream", said by someone already building it), warm, certain, inviting.
"We are building a city — a better blueprint for how we live."

## Recurring pieces

- **The day-stamp:** "Day 12, Munich, 7:40, rain" — the clock, every episode.
- **The goal card:** the one thing this episode builds.
- **The demo:** the thing running, in the worlds, at the end.
- **The founders:** thanked by name, every episode.
- **The open door:** what comes next — and an invitation to join.
